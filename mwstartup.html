How to Get Started

Follow these simple steps to set up your decentralized wallet and activate your smart contract levels safely[cite: 1].

1

Download Wallet

Install the TokenPocket Wallet app on your mobile device from the Google Play Store or Apple App Store[cite: 1].

2

Setup & Secure

Create a Polygon (POL) account. Write down your secret recovery phrases in a physical jotter and store securely offline[cite: 1].

3

Fund Account

Deposit funds (POL/USDT) matching the entry cost of your preferred registration package[cite: 1].

4

Activate DApp

Paste your sponsor's registration link into the TokenPocket DApp browser to activate your matrix levels[cite: 1].
